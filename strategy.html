The 6-step attack — run it on every question
1
Read the question before the options

Name the topic area, then who the client is, and whose duty is in play.

2
Find the operative word

BEST, MOST, FIRST, NOT, EXCEPT, ALWAYS. The decision hides in one word.

3
Predict the answer

Decide what a careful California broker would do before reading A–D.

4
Eliminate the two worst

Absolute words, other-state rules, or actions that favor the agent over the client.

5
Choose protective of the client and the public

Between the last two, pick the option that discloses more, documents more or protects client money.

6
Check the math twice

Rerun the arithmetic in reverse and check units (monthly vs. annual).

Playbook by question type
Best / Most118 in bank
Winning move: Pick the answer that protects the client, discloses fully and follows the written rule.
Distractors are partly true but incomplete.
NOT / Except10 in bank
Winning move: Restate it as three true statements and one false. Find the false one.
You choose a true statement because you forgot the NOT.
Sequence1 in bank
Winning move: Place events along a timeline and check which must precede which.
Foreclosure steps and disclosure timing are swapped in distractors.
Role ID1 in bank
Winning move: Name the roles: trustor, trustee, beneficiary, principal, agent, optionor.
Trustor and beneficiary are easy to swap.
Distinction28 in bank
Winning move: If options are near-neighbors, the question is the difference. Recite each definition.
Synonym-bait: void/voidable, commingling/conversion, steering/blockbusting.
Recall / Math28 in bank
Winning move: Anchor on the known number or formula and compute step by step.
Wrong units, multiplying instead of dividing, or forgetting the lower-of rule.

Six rules that decide close calls
Client money is sacred
Deposit within 3 business days, never commingle, never decide a deposit dispute.
Disclosure beats silence
Known material facts get disclosed whether or not the buyer asks.
Writing wins
Listings, land sales and commission claims depend on a signed writing.
Broker is responsible
Listings belong to the broker and the broker supervises.
Equal treatment always
Any action that sorts people by protected class is wrong.
Courts, not self-help
Evictions and deposit disputes go through lawful process.
